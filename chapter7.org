* Chapter 7: Understanding the Processor

- Goal of the chapter is to understand how atomics work at the
  processor level.
- Focuses on two processor architectures:
  - x86-64: The 64 bit version of x86 architecture. Also called as
    AMD64.
  - ARM64: The 64-bit version of the ARM architecture used by nearly
    all modern mobile devices, high performance embedded systems. Also
    known as AArch64.
